Dopamine Ratt — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

Dopamine Ratt collects nothing, sends nothing, and cannot reach the internet. This page explains what that means in detail, and how you can check it for yourself rather than take our word for it.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

The app does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. On Android, an app without that permission is placed outside the networking group by the operating system, so it cannot open a network connection at all. This is enforced by the system, not by our code: even a bug or a misbehaving library could not transmit anything.

What the app stores, and where

WhatWhereLeaves the device?
Your list of watched apps Private app storage on your phone (SharedPreferences) No
An optional image you supply as the sign artwork The app's own folder, only if you place a file there yourself No

Automatic backup is switched off (allowBackup="false"), and the app's data-extraction rules exclude its files from Google cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your watchlist does not travel to a new phone or into any backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

The accessibility service

Dopamine Ratt works by noticing when a watched app comes to the foreground, so it can show the interruption screen first. Android exposes that through an accessibility service, which is why the app asks you to enable one.

The service is declared with canRetrieveWindowContent="false". It therefore cannot read the contents of your screen: not text, not messages, not passwords, not what you type, not what you browse. It receives window-change events and reads a single field from them, the package name of the app that just came to the front, for example com.instagram.android. If that package is on the list you chose, the app shows its own screen. That is the entire mechanism.

Nothing about which apps you open, or when, is recorded, stored or timed. The package name is compared against your list and discarded.

The app requests no other permissions. It has no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, call logs, messages or accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Verifying this yourself

The complete source is published at github.com/mayilian/dopamine-ratt. You can build it yourself and compare the result with the published release. You can also inspect any release directly: the permission list in the APK shows that INTERNET is absent, and searching the compiled code turns up no networking classes and no server addresses.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it can access, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
